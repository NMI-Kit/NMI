# Math, Grade 11, 4 units (4 יח"ל)

Source: the recommendations of the math inspector for 2026-2027 (תשפ"ז).

## Instructions for the teacher about this guide

- The order of the topics is a recommendation, and the teacher is allowed to change it. Ask the student how far the class has got.
- Teach only the topics in this guide and the topics of grade 10.
- Angles in degrees. The answers are exact unless the question asked for an approximation.
- At most one parameter in integral problems.
- Write the term in English, and the first time put the Hebrew term in parentheses.
- The common mistakes in this guide come from general teaching experience, not from the Ministry.

## The exam

Form (שאלון) 35471 in grade 11.

## The topics in order

### m11-4-01 Probability (הסתברות)
- **Content:** completing the material of grade 10 as needed.
- **Out of scope:** three-stage events (מאורעות תלת-שלביים).

### m11-4-02 Pre-analysis of rational functions (קדם אנליזה של פונקציות רציונליות)
- **Content:** properties of functions of the form 1/g(x), where g is a polynomial of degree 2 at most.

### m11-4-03 Statistics (סטטיסטיקה)
- **Content:** completing the material of grade 10: measures of center and spread, and combining probability and statistics.

### m11-4-04 The normal distribution (התפלגות נורמלית)
- **Content:** the standard score (ציון תקן), graphical representation of different distributions, properties of the normal curve, the curve of standard scores, using the standard normal curve to find probabilities, standard scores, means and standard deviations.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting that the total area under the curve = 1. Reading the table in the wrong direction.

### m11-4-05 Differentiating rational functions (גזירת פונקציות רציונליות)
- **Content:** derivatives and applications: tangent problems (בעיות משיק), function investigation (חקירת פונקציה), the relation between the graph of the function and the graph of the derivative, extreme value problems with rational functions, and a review of extreme value problems for polynomials.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting the points where the function is not defined in the sign table.

### m11-4-06 Linear regression (רגרסיה לינארית)
- **Content:** a statistical relation between two variables (a scatter plot, דיאגרמת פיזור), the correlation coefficient (מקדם מתאם), the regression line.
- **Common mistakes:** treating correlation as causation.

### m11-4-07 Summary questions (שאלות סיכום)

### m11-4-08 Differentiating root functions (גזירת פונקציות שורש)
- **Content:** a review of root functions (a composition of a first degree expression and polynomials up to second degree), tangent, function investigation, the relation between the function and its derivative.

### m11-4-09 Analytic geometry: the circle (גאומטריה אנליטית - מעגל)
- **Content:** the equation of a circle, the relative position of a line and a circle.

### m11-4-10 Plane geometry: the circle (המעגל)
- **Content:** the chord theorems (מיתרים), central and inscribed angles, completing the perpendicular bisector theorems from grade 10, a triangle inscribed in a circle, a quadrilateral inscribed in a circle.

### m11-4-11 Trigonometry (טריגונומטריה)
- **Content:** the law of sines (משפט הסינוסים), combining it with the triangles of grade 10 and with analytic geometry.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting the case in which there are two solutions for the angle.

### m11-4-12 Integral calculus (חשבון אינטגרלי)
- **Content:** the antiderivative (פונקציה קדומה) and the indefinite integral of polynomials and rational functions in the specified form, finding a function from its derivative and a point, the definite integral and areas, at most one parameter.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting the constant C. An area under the axis without the absolute value.

### m11-4-13 Tangent to a circle (משיק למעגל)

### m11-4-14 Summary of the circle across all areas of geometry (סיכום המעגל בכל תחומי הגאומטריה)
- **Content:** combining analytic geometry, trigonometry and plane geometry in circle problems.
