# Exam-style examples, Grade 11, 4 units

Each example here is one question in the **format** of the exam questions: a story (or a function directly, like the differential calculus questions in the exam), parts a b c, and a "justify" part. The stories and the numbers are new, not copied from any exam. The numbers of the examples were checked with sympy (tests/kit/example_solutions_11.py).

## m11-4-01 Probability (הסתברות)

**The story:** in a class of 100 students, 60 of them are girls and 40 are boys. 45 girls and 20 boys play sports at school. A student is chosen at random.

**Part a (5 marks):** find the probability that the student is a girl given that they play sports.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the probability that they play sports given that they are a girl.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** are "the student is a girl" and "the student plays sports" independent events? Justify with a calculation.

## m11-4-02 Pre-analysis of rational functions (קדם אנליזה של פונקציות רציונליות)

**The story:** a question without a story, like the function investigation questions in the exam: the function f(x) = 1 / (x² - 4).

**Part a (4 marks):** find the domain of f.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the vertical and horizontal asymptotes.

**Part c (5 marks):** find the intervals where f is positive and the intervals where it is negative.

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** is f even, odd, or neither? Justify.

## m11-4-03 Statistics (סטטיסטיקה)

**The story:** the number of hours of watching television per week for 8 students: 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9.

**Part a (5 marks):** find the mean, the median and the mode.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the standard deviation.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** after a week, every student added two hours of watching. What happens to the mean and to the standard deviation? Justify.

## m11-4-04 The normal distribution (התפלגות נורמלית)

**The story:** the marks of a test are normally distributed, with a mean of 70 and a standard deviation of 10.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the standard score of a student whose mark is 85, and of a student whose mark is 60.

**Part b (5 marks):** approximately what percent of the students have marks between 60 and 80? (use the table of the standard normal distribution)

**Part c (6 marks) - justify:** in another test the mean is 80 and the standard deviation is 10, and a student has a mark of 90. Which mark is better relative to its class: 85 in the first test or 90 in the second? Justify.

## m11-4-05 Differentiating rational functions (גזירת פונקציות רציונליות)

**The story:** a rectangular garden whose area is 4 square meters. Its width is x m, and its length in meters is determined by the area.

**Part a (4 marks):** write the length in terms of x, and then write the perimeter P(x) in terms of x.

**Part b (6 marks):** find P'(x) and its zero in the domain x > 0.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** find the smallest possible perimeter. Justify that it is a minimum value, and what is the shape of the garden then?

## m11-4-06 Linear regression (רגרסיה לינארית)

**The story:** a table with the number of hours of study x and the test mark y of 5 students: (1, 55), (2, 60), (3, 70), (4, 75), (5, 90).

**Part a (6 marks):** find the equation of the regression line y = ax + b.

**Part b (4 marks):** find the expected mark of a student who studied 6 hours.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** the student's friend said: "With 10 hours of study the mark will be 129.5". Why is this prediction not reasonable? And does a high correlation coefficient mean that studying is the only cause of the mark?

## m11-4-07 Summary questions (שאלות סיכום)

**The story:** the marks of 20 students in an exam: 60 for two students, 70 for 6 students, 80 for 8 students, and 90 for 4 students.

**Part a (5 marks):** find the mean and the median.

**Part b (5 marks):** a student is chosen at random. Find the probability that their mark is 80 or more, and find the probability that their mark is 90 given that it is 80 or more.

**Part c (4 marks) - justify:** why is the median greater than the mean in this data?

## m11-4-08 Differentiating root functions (גזירת פונקציות שורש)

**The story:** a question without a story: the function f(x) = √(2x + 1).

**Part a (3 marks):** find the domain of f.

**Part b (6 marks):** find f'(x), and then the equation of the tangent to the curve f at x = 4.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** does f have a local extreme value? Justify with the sign of the derivative.

## m11-4-09 Analytic geometry: the circle (גאומטריה אנליטית - מעגל)

**The story:** a broadcast station at the point (3, 4) on the map, and its coverage is a circle with a radius of 5 km. The origin (0, 0) is a city.

**Part a (4 marks):** write the equation of the border of the coverage.

**Part b (4 marks):** is the city (0, 0) on the border of the coverage? Justify.

**Part c (5 marks):** find the intersection points of the border of the coverage with the x axis, and the length of the part of the axis that is inside the coverage.

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** a line whose equation is y = 9. Does it cross the circle, and at how many points? Justify.

## m11-4-10 Plane geometry: the circle (המעגל)

**The story:** a circular plaza whose center is O. On its rim there are 4 points A and B and C and D in this order. The inscribed angle ACB = 50° and the angle DAB = 80°.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the central angle AOB, and justify.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the angle BCD, and justify.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** if the angle DAB = 80° and the angle BCD = 110°, can ABCD be inscribed in a circle? Justify.

## m11-4-11 Trigonometry (טריגונומטריה)

**The story:** in a triangle ABC the angle A = 30° and the side opposite it a = 5 cm.

**Part a (5 marks):** if the angle B = 45°, find b in exact form, and find the angle C.

**Part b (5 marks):** if b = 8 cm instead of the angle B, find the angle B (to the nearest tenth of a degree).

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** why are there two possible answers for the angle B in part b?

## m11-4-12 Integral calculus (חשבון אינטגרלי)

**The story:** a car whose speed in meters per second is v(t) = 2t + 1, and it starts from the starting point (s(0) = 0).

**Part a (5 marks):** find the distance function s(t) and the distance after 4 seconds.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the definite integral from 0 to 3 of the function g(x) = x² - 4.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** find the area enclosed between the curve of g and the x axis between x = 0 and x = 3. Why is it not the same answer as in part b?

## m11-4-13 Tangent to a circle (משיק למעגל)

**The story:** a circle whose equation is (x - 1)² + (y - 2)² = 25, and the point P(4, 6) is on it.

**Part a (4 marks):** check that P is on the circle, and find the slope of the radius from the center to P.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the equation of the tangent to the circle at P.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** find the intersection points of the tangent with the two axes. Why is the slope of the tangent the reciprocal of the slope of the radius with the opposite sign?

## m11-4-14 Summary of the circle across all areas of geometry (סיכום המעגל בכל תחומי הגאומטריה)

**The story:** a circle centered at O(0, 0) with a radius of 10, and the points A(6, 8) and B(-6, 8) are on it.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the length of the chord AB.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the area of the triangle OAB.

**Part c (5 marks):** find the angle AOB with the law of cosines (to the nearest tenth of a degree).

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** why does the perpendicular from O to the chord AB bisect it?

## m11-4-15 Completions and review (השלמות וחזרות)

**The story:** a straight street whose equation is y = x - 2, and a curved street whose equation is y = x² - 6x + 8.

**Part a (5 marks):** find the intersection points of the two streets.

**Part b (4 marks):** find the distance between the two intersection points.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** if the straight street were y = x - 20, would it cross the curved street? Justify with the discriminant.
