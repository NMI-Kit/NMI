# Exam-style examples, Grade 11, 3 units

Each example here is one question in the **format** of the exam questions: a story, parts a b c, and a "justify" part. The stories and the numbers are new, not copied from any exam. The numbers of the examples were checked with sympy (tests/kit/example_solutions_11.py).

## m11-3-01 Exponential operations: growth and decay (גדילה ודעיכה)

**The story:** a village has 2,000 residents, and its population grows by 10% every year.

**Part a (4 marks):** how many residents will the village have after a year?

**Part b (6 marks):** how many residents will it have after 3 years? Write the factor you multiply by every year.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** a student said: "After 3 years the number grew by 30%, because 3 × 10% = 30%, so it will be 2,600". Where is the mistake?

## m11-3-02 Statistics: standard deviation (סטיית תקן)

**The story:** two classes took the same test. The marks of 4 students from the first class: 70, 70, 90, 90. The marks of 4 students from the second class: 78, 78, 82, 82.

**Part a (5 marks):** find the mean mark of each class.

**Part b (6 marks):** find the standard deviation of each class.

**Part c (4 marks) - justify:** the mean is the same in both classes. In which class are the marks closer to each other? Justify.

## m11-3-03 Probability (הסתברות)

**The story:** in a box there are 10 balls: 6 red and 4 blue. Two balls are drawn, one after the other, without putting the first back in the box.

**Part a (5 marks):** find the probability that both balls are red.

**Part b (6 marks):** find the probability that the two balls are of different colors.

**Part c (4 marks) - justify:** a student calculated the probability that both balls are red like this: 0.6 × 0.6 = 0.36. Why is his calculation wrong?

## m11-3-04 Ratio, proportion, scale and unit conversion (יחס, פרופורציה, קנה מידה והמרת יחידות)

**The story:** on a map whose scale is 1:50,000, the distance between two villages is 6 cm, and the area of a lake is 4 square cm.

**Part a (4 marks):** how many kilometers is the real distance between the villages?

**Part b (6 marks):** how many square kilometers is the real area of the lake?

**Part c (4 marks) - justify:** a student calculated the area of the lake like this: 4 × 50,000 = 200,000 square cm. Where is the mistake?

## m11-3-05 Similarity of triangles (דמיון משולשים)

**The story:** an advertising sign in the shape of a triangle ABC, and a larger copy of it is made in the shape of a triangle DEF. The triangles are similar. AB = 8 cm and DE = 12 cm. The perimeter of ABC = 30 cm, and its altitude to the side AB = 10 cm, and its area is 40 square cm.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the similarity ratio between DEF and ABC.

**Part b (4 marks):** find the perimeter of DEF and the corresponding altitude to DE.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** find the area of DEF. A student said it is 60 square cm because the ratio is 3/2. Where is the mistake?

## m11-3-06 Using geometric knowledge (שימוש בידע גאומטרי)

**The story:** the roof of a house in the shape of an isosceles triangle ABC (AB = AC) and the apex angle A = 40°. The segment DE is parallel to the base BC, and the point D is on AB and the point E is on AC.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the two angles B and C.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the angle ADE, and justify.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** find the angle DEC, and state the reason.

## m11-3-07 Properties of quadrilaterals (תכונות של מרובעים)

**The story:** a plot of land in the shape of a trapezoid whose bases are 12 m and 8 m, and the height between them is 5 m. The price of a square meter is 40 ₪.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the area of the land.

**Part b (4 marks):** how much will all the land cost?

**Part c (6 marks) - justify:** we connect the midpoints of the legs with a line. Find its length. A student said that this line splits the area into two equal parts. Is he right? Justify with a calculation.

## m11-3-08 Trigonometry (טריגונומטריה)

**The story:** a ladder 10 m long leans against a wall, and touches the wall at a height of 8 m.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the distance of the bottom of the ladder from the wall.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the angle between the ladder and the ground to the nearest tenth of a degree.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** for safety the angle with the ground must be at least 60°. Is the ladder safe? And what must the distance of its bottom from the wall be so that the angle is 60°?

## m11-3-09 Exponential operations: growth and decay in a financial context (גדילה ודעיכה בהקשר פיננסי)

**The story:** Husam put 10,000 ₪ in a savings account at an interest of 10% every year (the interest is added to the amount). And a car whose price is 50,000 ₪ loses 20% of its value every year.

**Part a (5 marks):** how much will the amount in the savings account be after two years?

**Part b (5 marks):** how much will the value of the car be after two years?

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** his friend said: "After 5 years the car loses 100% of its value, because 5 × 20% = 100%". Is he right? Justify.

## m11-3-10 Statistics: standard deviation in a financial context (סטיית תקן בהקשר פיננסי)

**The story:** two branches of a store pay wages (in shekels per month). The wages of 4 employees in the first branch: 5,000, 5,000, 7,000, 7,000. The wages of 4 employees in the second branch: 6,000, 6,000, 6,000, 6,000.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the mean wage and the standard deviation in the first branch.

**Part b (4 marks):** find the mean wage and the standard deviation in the second branch.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** the mean is the same in both branches. What does the standard deviation say about the difference between them?

## m11-3-11 Review and practice, completing grade 10 topics as needed (חזרה ותרגול, עם השלמת נושאי כיתה י׳ לפי הצורך)

**The story:** the price of a jacket is 200 ₪. The store raised its price by 10%, and a week later gave a discount of 10%.

**Part a (4 marks):** what did the price become after the raise?

**Part b (4 marks):** what did the price become after the discount?

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** why is the last price not 200 ₪ even though they raised 10% and lowered 10%?
