# Math, Grade 11, 3 units (3 יח"ל)

Source: the recommendations of the math inspector for 2026-2027 (תשפ"ז), the new curriculum with clusters.

## Instructions for the teacher about this guide

- The curriculum is built on clusters (אשכולות), and all the topics are from real life. Always use examples from real life.
- All the clusters need skills from grade 10. If the student is missing something from grade 10, take them back to it.
- The order is a recommendation. Ask the student where the class has reached.
- Write the term in English, and the first time put the Hebrew term in parentheses.
- The common mistakes in this guide come from general teaching experience, not from the Ministry.

## The exam

The Bagrut at the end of grade 11: form (שאלון) 35371, and it covers all the topics of grades 10 and 11. That means the student needs to review the material of grade 10 too.

## Outside the 3-unit curriculum

Sequences, trigonometry in space, differential and integral calculus, and the analytic geometry of the circle (according to the new curriculum document). If the student asks about them, tell them honestly that they are not in the 3-unit curriculum.

## The topics in order

### Science and Society cluster (אשכול מדע וחברה)

#### m11-3-01 Exponential operations: growth and decay (גדילה ודעיכה)
- **Content:** growth and decay problems: a quantity that increases or decreases at a constant rate over time.
- **Common mistakes:** mixing up the growth rate (5%) and the factor (1.05). Using a growth factor in a decay problem.

#### m11-3-02 Statistics: standard deviation (סטיית תקן)
- **Content:** the standard deviation, and combining measures of center and spread for conclusions.
- **Possible (not certain):** quartiles and deciles (רבעונים ועשירונים), which are in the curriculum document and not in the recommendations.
- **Common mistakes:** relying on the mean alone to compare two groups.

#### m11-3-03 Probability (הסתברות)
- **Content:** extending grade 10: the probability of the intersection of two independent and dependent events, and the probability of an event built from a union and an intersection of events whose probabilities are known.
- **Common mistakes:** multiplying the probabilities of dependent events as if they were independent.

### Orientation in the plane and in space cluster (אשכול התמצאות במישור ובמרחב)

#### m11-3-04 Ratio, proportion, scale and unit conversion (יחס, פרופורציה, קנה מידה והמרת יחידות)
- **Content:** ratio and proportion, and the scale of maps and drawings, and unit conversion.
- **Common mistakes:** converting units of area with the same factor as length (1 m² = 100 cm² is wrong, the correct one is 10,000).

#### m11-3-05 Similarity of triangles (דמיון משולשים)
- **Content:** the similarity theorem (AA), the similarity ratio, the ratio of perimeters and areas.
- **Common mistakes:** the ratio of areas = the similarity ratio instead of its square.

#### m11-3-06 Using geometric knowledge (שימוש בידע גאומטרי)
- **Content:** the sum of the angles of a triangle, vertical angles, angles between parallel lines, the properties of the isosceles, the equilateral and the right triangle.

#### m11-3-07 Properties of quadrilaterals (תכונות של מרובעים)
- **Content:** the parallelogram and its special cases, the trapezoid and its special cases, the kite (דלתון), unit conversion.

#### m11-3-08 Trigonometry (טריגונומטריה)
- **Content:** sin and cos and tan of an acute angle in a right triangle, calculating sides, angles, perimeters and areas, angle of elevation and angle of depression, splitting shapes into right triangles.
- **Common mistakes:** the calculator in radians. Mixing up the opposite side and the adjacent side.

### Financial-economic cluster (אשכול פיננסי-כלכלי)

#### m11-3-09 Exponential operations: growth and decay in a financial context (גדילה ודעיכה בהקשר פיננסי)
- **Content:** growth and decay problems: a quantity that increases or decreases at a constant rate over time.

#### m11-3-10 Statistics: standard deviation in a financial context (סטיית תקן בהקשר פיננסי)
- **Content:** the standard deviation on financial data.

#### m11-3-11 Review and practice, completing grade 10 topics as needed (חזרה ותרגול, עם השלמת נושאי כיתה י׳ לפי הצורך)

## Teaching tips from the Ministry
- One main topic in each period, and two topics in parallel are possible.
- After two topics are finished, a weekly review lesson.
