# Math, Grade 11, 5 units (5 יח"ל)

Source: the recommendations of the math inspector for 2026-2027 (תשפ"ז), the new curriculum.

## Instructions for the teacher about this guide

- The order of the topics is a recommendation, and the teacher is allowed to change it. Ask the student where the class has reached.
- Teach only the topics in this guide and the topics of grade 10. If they ask about something from grade 12, tell them it is in grade 12.
- The answers are exact (roots, π, fractions), unless the question asked for an approximation.
- Write the term in English, and the first time put the Hebrew term in parentheses.
- The common mistakes in this guide come from general teaching experience, not from the Ministry.

## The exam

According to the table of exam rules for 2026-2027: the 5-unit exam forms (שאלון) are 35571 and 35572. Which grade takes which form is not verified (the teaching recommendations do not say), so ask the teacher.

## The topics in order

### m11-5-01 Trigonometric functions (פונקציות טריגונומטריות)
- **Content:** completing the material of grade 10 as needed (the unit circle, the law of sines and the law of cosines), trigonometric identities (זהויות טריגונומטריות), trigonometric equations, and using them in geometry problems.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting solutions of the equation. Dividing by an expression that may equal zero.

### m11-5-02 Differential calculus (חשבון דיפרנציאלי)
- **Content:** the rate of change, the second derivative (נגזרת שנייה), concavity (קעירות), inflection points (נקודות פיתול), the relation between the graph of the function and the graphs of its first and second derivative.
- **Rational and root functions:** behavior near points of non-definition and at the ends of the domain, vertical asymptotes, a removable discontinuity (אי רציפות סליקה), families of functions with a parameter, tangent problems.
- **Investigating functions** built from division, multiplication, addition, subtraction and composition.
- **Common mistakes:** f''(x)=0 is not always an inflection point. Forgetting the domain before the investigation.

### m11-5-03 Sequences (סדרות)
- **Content:** a general sequence, the geometric sequence (סדרה הנדסית): the general term, the recursive rule (כלל נסיגה), properties, a partial sum, the infinite sum when it converges, subsequences, and integer exponents and exponential equations by trial.
- **Common mistakes:** using the infinite sum formula when |q| ≥ 1.

### m11-5-04 Extreme value problems (בעיות קיצון)
- **Content:** completing and extending grade 10: extrema with root and rational functions, on a closed and an open interval, problems from a graph and from plane geometry.
- **Common mistakes:** not checking that the point really is an extremum. Forgetting the limits of the problem on the variable.

### m11-5-05 Mathematical induction (אינדוקציה מתמטית)
- **Content:** the principle of induction, proving sums, proving the equivalence of different representations of a sequence, telling a correct proof from wrong steps.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting the base step. Using the assumption without reaching the case n+1.

### m11-5-06 Differentiating trigonometric functions (גזירת פונקציות טריגונומטריות)
- **Content:** properties, graphs and operations, derivatives of trigonometric functions and their applications, tangent, investigating a sum, product and quotient of trigonometric functions, composing polynomials and roots with trigonometric functions with a linear variable, extreme value problems.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting the chain rule with sin(ax+b). The calculator in degrees instead of radians.

### m11-5-07 Probability (הסתברות)
- **Content:** the sample space, events and operations on them, the laws of probability, conditional probability (הסתברות מותנית), a two-way table (2×2 or 2×3), a probability tree, independent and dependent events, the Bernoulli formula (נוסחת ברנולי).
- **Common mistakes:** mixing up P(A|B) and P(B|A). Forgetting the binomial coefficient in the Bernoulli formula.

### m11-5-08 Integral calculus (חשבון אינטגרלי)
- **Content:** the antiderivative (פונקציה קדומה), the area accumulation function as the reverse of differentiation, the relation between the graph of the function, its derivative and the accumulation function, the integral of polynomials, roots and trigonometric functions (with identities), rules: multiplying by a constant, addition and subtraction, composition with a linear inner function, and multiplying a composite function by the derivative of the inner one.
- **Applications:** finding a function from its derivative and a point, and calculating areas.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting the constant C. Calculating an area under the axis as positive without the absolute value.
