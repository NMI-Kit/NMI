# Glossary: Arabic | Hebrew | English (math and exam terms of the kit)

One list for the three languages of the prompt. The Hebrew is the term of the Israeli Ministry of Education (and of the topic names in the title lines of the level files). In the English prompt, the first time a term appears the Hebrew term follows it in parentheses, for example "law of sines (משפט הסינוסים)". The Arabic prompt is the original and is not changed by this file.
