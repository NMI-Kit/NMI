# Bagrut-style examples, Grade 10, 3 units

Each example here is one question in the **format** of the Bagrut questions: a story, parts a b c, and a "justify" part. The stories and the numbers are new, not copied from any exam. The numbers of the examples were checked with sympy (tests/kit/example_solutions.py).

## m10-3-01 Drawing conclusions from information (הסקת מסקנות ממידע)

**The story:** a school runs after-school activities, and this is a table with the number of participants in each activity: football 45, theater 30, drawing 15, music 30. The total number of participants is 120 students.

**Part a (5 marks):** what percent of the participants are in theater?

**Part b (6 marks):** we want to show the results in a pie chart. How many degrees is the angle of the sector for football?

**Part c (6 marks) - justify:** 10 new students joined music, and the other activities did not change. Did the share of participants in football go up, go down, or stay the same? Justify with a calculation.

## m10-3-02 Statistical representations (ייצוגים סטטיסטיים)

**The story:** a teacher recorded the marks of 20 students in a short quiz (out of 10). The table shows the mark and the number of students who got it: 5 (two students), 6 (3 students), 7 (6 students), 8 (5 students), 9 (3 students), 10 (one student).

**Part a (5 marks):** find the relative frequency, in percent, of the mark 7.

**Part b (6 marks):** what percent of the students got 8 or more?

**Part c (6 marks) - justify:** a student said: "The mode is 10 because it is the highest mark". Is he right? Justify.

## m10-3-03 Statistics (סטטיסטיקה)

**The story:** the wages of five workers in a restaurant (in shekels per month): 4,000, 4,200, 4,200, 4,600, 7,000.

**Part a (6 marks):** find the mean, the median and the mode of the wages.

**Part b (5 marks) - justify:** the owner of the restaurant says "the mean wage at my place is 4,800". Which measure gives a more honest picture of the wage of a regular worker? Justify.

**Part c (6 marks):** the restaurant also employs 3 workers in another kitchen, and the mean of their wages is 3,600. Find the mean wage of all eight workers.

## m10-3-04 Probability (הסתברות)

**The story:** in a box there are 12 balls: 5 red, 4 blue and 3 green. One ball is drawn at random.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the probability that it is red.

**Part b (4 marks):** find the probability that it is not green.

**Part c (6 marks):** a ball is drawn, its color is recorded, it is put back in the box, and a second ball is drawn. How many ordered pairs (the first color, the second color) are possible? And find the probability that the first is red and the second is blue.

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** is the probability of (red then blue) equal to the probability of (blue then red)? Justify.

## m10-3-05 Combining statistics and probability (שילוב סטטיסטיקה והסתברות)

**The story:** a football team recorded the number of goals in 20 games: 0 goals in 4 games, one goal in 5 games, two goals in 6 games, 3 goals in 3 games, and 4 goals in two games.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the mean number of goals per game.

**Part b (4 marks):** find the median number of goals.

**Part c (5 marks):** a game is chosen at random. Find the probability that the team scored 3 goals or more in it.

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** the coach said: "In more than half of the games we scored at least two goals". Is he right? Justify with a calculation.

## m10-3-06 Perimeters in everyday contexts (היקפים בהקשר אורייני)

**The story:** a yard in the shape of a rectangle, 30 m long and 20 m wide. On the width side a semicircle with a diameter of 20 m was added (a small garden), and the semicircle is outside the rectangle.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the perimeter of the semicircle (leave π in the answer).

**Part b (5 marks):** find the perimeter of the whole shape (the rectangle with the semicircle, without the shared diameter). Leave π in the answer.

**Part c (5 marks):** they want to put a fence around the shape, and every meter costs 25 ₪. How much will the fence cost? (π = 3.14)

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** why do we not count the diameter (20 m) in the length of the fence?

## m10-3-07 Distance, speed and time (מסלולים)

**The story:** Sami rides his bicycle to school 6 km at a speed of 12 km/h, and returns by the same road at a speed of 8 km/h.

**Part a (4 marks):** how many minutes does Sami take going there?

**Part b (4 marks):** how many minutes does he take coming back?

**Part c (6 marks):** find Sami's average speed for the whole trip (there and back).

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** his friend said: "The average speed is 10 km/h, because (12+8)/2=10". Why is his calculation wrong?

## m10-3-08 Areas (שטחים)

**The story:** a plot of land in the shape of a right triangle, its two legs are 12 m and 16 m.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the length of the hypotenuse.

**Part b (4 marks):** find the area of the land.

**Part c (5 marks):** they want to plant grass on all the land, and every square meter costs 35 ₪. How much will the planting cost?

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** a student calculated the area like this: 20 × 12 ÷ 2 = 120 square meters. Where is the mistake?

## m10-3-09 Tiling (ריצופים)

**The story:** a rectangular living room 6 m long and 4 m wide, and they want to tile it with square tiles whose side is 50 cm.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the area of the living room in square meters.

**Part b (5 marks):** how many tiles cover the living room?

**Part c (5 marks):** a tile costs 18 ₪, and they want to buy 5 extra tiles as a reserve. How much will all the tiles cost?

**Part d (5 marks) - justify:** the seller said: "If you choose tiles whose side is 25 cm (half), you will need double the number". Is he right? Justify.

## m10-3-10 Combining the topics of the cluster (שילוב נושאי האשכול)

**The story:** a rectangular field 40 m long and 30 m wide. A runner runs around it at a constant speed of 4 meters per second.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the perimeter of the field.

**Part b (4 marks):** how many seconds does the runner take to complete one lap?

**Part c (5 marks):** how many full laps does he complete in 7 minutes?

**Part d (5 marks) - justify:** if the length and the width of the field are doubled, are the perimeter and the area doubled? Justify.

## m10-3-11 Drawing conclusions from financial information (הסקת מסקנות ממידע פיננסי)

**The story:** a clothing store recorded the sales and the expenses (in shekels) for three months: January: sales 12,000 and expenses 9,000. February: sales 15,000 and expenses 10,500. March: sales 14,000 and expenses 11,200.

**Part a (5 marks):** find the profit in each month (profit = sales - expenses).

**Part b (5 marks):** find the profit as a share of the sales in February.

**Part c (6 marks) - justify:** the owner of the store said: "March was better than January because its sales are higher". Is the conclusion right in terms of profit? Justify.

## m10-3-12 The linear model (מודל לינארי)

**The story:** two taxi companies. The first charges 10 ₪ to start the meter, and then 4 ₪ for every kilometer. The second charges 5 ₪ for every kilometer with no starting charge.

**Part a (4 marks):** write an equation for the fare y (in shekels) of each company, in terms of the number of kilometers x.

**Part b (5 marks):** for what distance is the fare the same in both companies? And what is the fare?

**Part c (4 marks) - justify:** explain in words what the number 4 means in the equation of the first company.

**Part d (4 marks):** a trip of 14 km. Which company is cheaper, and by how much?

## m10-3-13 Word problems (שאלות מילוליות)

**The story:** a merchant bought 50 shirts, each shirt for 80 ₪. He sold 30 shirts at a price of 120 ₪ each. And he gave a discount of 20% off the price of 120 on the remaining shirts (20 shirts) and sold all of them.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the cost of buying all the shirts.

**Part b (4 marks):** find the price of a shirt after the discount.

**Part c (5 marks):** find the merchant's total profit.

**Part d (5 marks) - justify:** find the profit as a percent of the cost. And why do we calculate the profit from the cost, not from the selling price?

## m10-3-14 Statistics in a financial context (סטטיסטיקה בהקשר פיננסי)

**The story:** a store with two departments. The sales department has 4 employees and the mean of their monthly wage is 5,500 ₪. The accounting department has two employees and the mean of their wage is 7,000 ₪.

**Part a (5 marks):** find the mean wage of all six employees.

**Part b (5 marks) - justify:** a student calculated (5,500 + 7,000) ÷ 2 = 6,250. Why is his calculation wrong?

**Part c (6 marks):** the wages of the four sales employees are: 5,000, 5,000, 5,500, 6,500. Find the median and the mode.
