# Bagrut-style examples, Grade 10, 4 units

Each example here is one question in the **format** of the Bagrut questions: a story, parts a b c, and a "justify" part. The stories and the numbers are new, not copied from any exam. The numbers of the examples were checked with sympy (tests/kit/example_solutions.py).

## m10-4-01 Analytic geometry (גאומטריה אנליטית)

**The story:** on a map of a neighborhood, a school at point A(1, 2) and a playground at point B(7, 10). Every unit on the map = 100 meters.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the length of the segment AB, and then the real distance in kilometers.

**Part b (4 marks):** find the coordinates of M, the midpoint of AB.

**Part c (6 marks):** they want to build a road that passes through M and is perpendicular to AB. Find the equation of this road.

**Part d (5 marks) - justify:** is the point C(12, 0) on the road? And is it at the same distance from A and from B? Justify.

## m10-4-02 Introduction to function analysis and algebra (קדם אנליזה)

**The story:** a player threw a ball upward. From the moment of the throw until it lands on the ground, the height of the ball in meters after t seconds is h(t) = -5t² + 20t.

**Part a (4 marks):** after how many seconds does the ball return to the ground?

**Part b (5 marks):** find the maximum height the ball reaches, and when.

**Part c (6 marks):** during which period of time is the height of the ball greater than 15 meters? (solve the inequality h(t) > 15)

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** a student solved t² - 4t + 3 < 0 and wrote "t < 1 or t > 3". Where is the mistake?

## m10-4-03 Plane geometry: similarity (דמיון משולשים)

**The story:** the roof of a house in the shape of a triangle ABC. The point D is on the side AB, and the point E is on the side AC, and the segment DE is parallel to BC. The given data: AD = 6 m, DB = 3 m, BC = 15 m, and the area of the triangle ABC = 54 square meters.

**Part a (4 marks):** prove that the triangles ADE and ABC are similar, and find the similarity ratio.

**Part b (4 marks):** find the length of DE.

**Part c (5 marks):** find the area of the triangle ADE.

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** a student calculated the area of ADE like this: 54 × (2/3) = 36. Where is the mistake?

## m10-4-04 Functions: power functions and transformations (פונקציית חזקה)

**The story:** a computer game designer draws curves on the screen, and the origin (0, 0) is in the middle of the screen, so the coordinates can be negative. He starts from the function f(x) = x² and wants to make adjusted copies of it.

**Part a (4 marks):** the new path is g(x) = (x - 3)². In which direction and by how many units did the graph move from f(x) = x²?

**Part b (6 marks):** the path h(x) = -(x - 3)² + 4. Find the vertex, the direction in which the curve opens, and the intercepts with the x axis.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** is the function h even? Justify by substituting two values.

## m10-4-05 Trigonometry (טריגונומטריה)

**The story:** a ladder 5 m long leans against a wall, and makes an angle of 60° with the ground.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the height of the point where the ladder touches the wall (in exact form, without rounding).

**Part b (4 marks):** find the distance of the bottom of the ladder from the wall.

**Part c (5 marks):** check with Pythagoras that the height, the distance and the ladder satisfy the relation.

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** a student wrote sin 60° = -0.3048 on the calculator. What is the mistake, and how does he fix it?

## m10-4-06 Differential calculus (חשבון דיפרנציאלי)

**The story:** the height of a mountain road in hundreds of meters at a distance of x kilometers from its start is h(x) = x³ - 6x² + 9x, where 0 ≤ x ≤ 4.

**Part a (5 marks):** find h'(x) and the zeros of h'.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the intervals where it is increasing and decreasing, and the local extremum points with their values.

**Part c (5 marks):** find the highest and the lowest height on the whole road 0 ≤ x ≤ 4.

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** why must we check the value of h at x = 0 and x = 4 before we decide the highest and the lowest point?

## m10-4-07 Plane geometry: special lines in a triangle (קווים מיוחדים במשולש)

**The story:** a garden in the shape of a triangle ABC with a right angle at vertex B. AB = 6 m, BC = 8 m, AC = 10 m. The point M is the midpoint of the hypotenuse AC, and the segment BM connects the vertex of the right angle with the midpoint of the hypotenuse.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the length of BM.

**Part b (5 marks):** the three medians meet at a point G. Find the length of BG.

**Part c (5 marks):** find the area of the triangle ABM.

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** a student said that BM is the altitude of the triangle to the hypotenuse AC. Is he right? Justify with a calculation.

## m10-4-08 The root function (פונקציית שורש)

**The story:** an engineer designs a ramp, and its height above the ground in meters is f(x) = √(x + 6). x is the horizontal distance in meters from a lamp post (x = 0 at the post, and negative before the post).

**Part a (3 marks):** find the domain of f.

**Part b (6 marks):** find f'(x), and then the equation of the tangent to the curve f at x = 3.

**Part c (5 marks):** solve the equation √(x + 6) = x.

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** why does the solution x = -2 come out when squaring but is rejected?

## m10-4-09 Statistics (סטטיסטיקה)

**The story:** a clinic recorded the waiting times (in minutes) of 10 patients: 3, 5, 6, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 14.

**Part a (5 marks):** find the mean, the median and the mode.

**Part b (6 marks):** find the range and the standard deviation (find the variance first).

**Part c (5 marks):** find the first quartile and the third quartile.

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** in another clinic the mean waiting time is also 8 minutes, and the standard deviation is 1 minute. Which clinic has more consistent waits? Justify.

## m10-4-10 Probability (הסתברות)

**The story:** a factory makes parts on two machines. Machine A makes 60% of the parts, and machine B makes 40%. The share of defective parts is 5% of the parts of A, and 10% of the parts of B. A part is chosen at random.

**Part a (4 marks):** draw the probability tree, and find the probability that the part is defective.

**Part b (6 marks):** the part turned out to be defective. Find the probability that it is from machine A.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** is P(A | defective) equal to P(defective | A)? Justify with numbers.

## m10-4-11 Combining statistics and probability (שילוב סטטיסטיקה והסתברות)

**The story:** in a spelling test of 40 students, the number of mistakes: 0 mistakes for 10 students, one mistake for 15 students, two mistakes for 10 students, and 3 mistakes for 5 students.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the mean number of mistakes.

**Part b (4 marks):** a student is chosen at random. Find the probability that he has two mistakes at most.

**Part c (6 marks):** two students are chosen at random without replacement. Find the probability that both have no mistakes at all.

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** the median number of mistakes is 1 and the mean is 1.25. Why is the mean greater than the median?
