# Math, Grade 10, 3 units (3 יח"ל)

Source: the official curriculum of the Ministry of Education (the new curriculum with clusters), and the recommendations of the math inspector for 2026-2027 (תשפ"ז).

## Instructions for the teacher about this guide

- The new 3-unit curriculum is built on three clusters (אשכולות), and all the topics are from real life: data, money, shapes and distances. Always use examples from real life.
- All the grade 10 topics must be covered, because grade 11 is built on them.
- The order is a recommendation. Ask the student how far the class has got.
- Algebra is learned when it is needed in a problem, not on its own.
- Write the term in English, and the first time put the Hebrew term in parentheses.
- The common mistakes in this guide come from general teaching experience, not from the Ministry.

## The exam

The teaching recommendations for 2026-2027 name form (שאלון) 35173 for grade 10, and the table of exam rules names 35172 (the latest published exam: 35172, winter 2026), so ask the teacher which form your school takes. The Bagrut at the end of grade 11 is form 35371, and it covers all the topics of grades 10 and 11. Grade 12: 35372.

## Outside the 3-unit curriculum

Sequences, trigonometry in space, differential and integral calculus, and the analytic geometry of the circle (according to the new curriculum document). If the student asks about them, tell them honestly that they are not in the 3-unit curriculum.

## The topics in order

### Science and Society cluster (אשכול מדע וחברה)

#### m10-3-01 Drawing conclusions from information (הסקת מסקנות ממידע)
- **Content:** reading bar charts, pie charts and graphs, functions in algebraic, verbal and graphical form.
- **Support:** first and second degree equations, and percentages.
- **Common mistakes:** reading the wrong axis. Calculating the percentage from the wrong number.

#### m10-3-02 Statistical representations (ייצוגים סטטיסטיים)
- **Content:** reading and building frequency tables, relative frequency, converting between a table and a chart.

#### m10-3-03 Statistics (סטטיסטיקה)
- **Content:** mode, median, mean, weighted average.
- **Common mistakes:** finding the median without sorting. Calculating a weighted average as a regular average.

#### m10-3-04 Probability (הסתברות)
- **Content:** probability as relative frequency, sample space, events, complementary event, mutually exclusive events, two-stage events as ordered pairs, union of mutually exclusive events.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting that the probabilities add up to 1. Missing some of the ordered pairs.

#### m10-3-05 Combining statistics and probability (שילוב סטטיסטיקה והסתברות)
- **Content:** problems that combine statistics and probability from the earlier topics.

### Orientation in the plane and in space cluster (אשכול התמצאות במישור ובמרחב)

#### m10-3-06 Perimeters in everyday contexts (היקפים בהקשר אורייני)
- **Content:** perimeter of polygons, circumference of a circle and its parts, the Pythagorean theorem, composite shapes.
- **Common mistakes:** mixing up the perimeter formula and the area formula of a circle.

#### m10-3-07 Distance, speed and time (מסלולים)
- **Content:** the formula distance = speed × time.
- **Common mistakes:** different units (minutes and hours, meters and kilometers).

#### m10-3-08 Areas (שטחים)
- **Content:** triangles, quadrilaterals, circles, composite shapes.
- **Common mistakes:** using a side instead of the height in the area of a triangle.

#### m10-3-09 Tiling (ריצופים)
- **Content:** covering an area with tiles, and calculating the number of tiles and the cost (the document only mentions the name, and the shape is from Ministry exams).

#### m10-3-10 Combining the topics of the cluster (שילוב נושאי האשכול)
- **Content:** problems that combine all the topics of the orientation in the plane and in space cluster.

### Financial-economic cluster (אשכול פיננסי-כלכלי)

#### m10-3-11 Drawing conclusions from financial information (הסקת מסקנות ממידע פיננסי)
- **Content:** analyzing data in charts, tables or algebraic expressions, and making a decision according to profitability.

#### m10-3-12 The linear model (מודל לינארי)
- **Content:** slope as a rate of change, parallel lines and what they mean, algebraic and graphical form of the linear model, the meaning of the coefficients in context.
- **Common mistakes:** not explaining the meaning of the slope in words (for example: "every hour of work adds 40 shekels").

#### m10-3-13 Word problems (שאלות מילוליות)
- **Content:** buying and selling, wages, profit and loss. The data is given in words, a table, a chart or an algebraic expression.
- **Common mistakes:** calculating the profit from the selling price instead of from the cost.

#### m10-3-14 Statistics in a financial context (סטטיסטיקה בהקשר פיננסי)
- **Content:** mode, median, mean, weighted average on financial data.

## Teaching tips from the Ministry
- One main topic in each period, and two topics in parallel are possible.
- After two topics are finished, a weekly review lesson on the topics that will appear in the exam.
