# Math, Grade 10, 5 units (5 יח"ל)

Source: the official curriculum of the Ministry of Education for grade 10, 5 units, and the recommendations of the math inspector for 2026-2027 (תשפ"ז).
Total hours according to the curriculum: 150 hours.

## Instructions for the teacher about this guide

- The order of the topics is the recommendation of the Ministry, and the teacher in the school is allowed to change it. Ask the student where the class has reached, and do not assume.
- Teach only the topics in this guide. If the student asks about something that was moved to grade 11, tell them it is in grade 11, and help them with a simple idea only if they ask.
- The answers are in exact form, not approximate: √2/2 and not 0.707, and π and not 3.14. Rounding only if the question asked for it.
- Write the term in English, and the first time put the Hebrew term in parentheses, because many books and papers are in Hebrew.
- Geometry problems need an orderly proof: every step has a reason (given, theorem, definition).
- It is forbidden to rely on a theorem that has not been learned yet. For example: Thales' theorem is not proved with similarity, because similarity is built on it.
- The common mistakes in this guide come from general teaching experience, not from the Ministry.

## The exam

According to the table of exam rules for 2026-2027: the 5-unit exam forms (שאלון) are 35571 and 35572. Which grade takes which form is not verified (the teaching recommendations do not say), so ask the teacher. Old Bagrut questions are useful for practice only on the topics in this guide.

## The topics in order

### m10-5-01 Introduction to analytic geometry (מבוא לגאומטריה אנליטית)
- **Content:** points and segments, the length of a segment, the midpoint of a segment, the equation of a line from two points or from a slope and a point, parallel and perpendicular lines, the intersection of lines, the explicit form and the general form of the line and the advantages of each.
- **Also:** systems of linear equations and their graphical representation with a simple parameter, quadratic-linear systems, the difference between the graph of a function and a set of points that is not a function (like x = k).
- **The circle:** the equation of a circle centered at the origin, and a circle as a locus from the distance formula, and shifting a circle.
- **Out of scope:** the general equation of the circle.
- **Common mistakes:** the product of the slopes = −1 only for perpendicular lines, and they forget that a vertical line has no slope. Mixing up the order of subtraction in the slope formula.

### m10-5-02 Introduction to function analysis (קדם אנליזה)
- **Content:** reading the graph of a function: intercepts with the axes, where it is positive and negative, increasing and decreasing, extremum points. A review of the linear and the quadratic function graphically and algebraically.
- **Common mistakes:** mixing up the extremum point and its value. Answering "where it is increasing" with y values instead of x.

### m10-5-03 Geometry: review and completions (גאומטריה: חזרה והשלמות)
- **Content:** a review of triangles and quadrilaterals from middle school, the midsegment in a triangle and in a trapezoid, and the meeting point of the medians (מפגש התיכונים) that splits each median in the ratio 2:1.
- **More from the curriculum:** loci (circle, perpendicular bisector, angle bisector, parallel lines), and the meeting of the perpendicular bisectors (the center of the circumscribed circle), and of the angle bisectors (the center of the inscribed circle), and the altitudes. The fourth congruence theorem (משפט חפיפה רביעי): two sides and the angle opposite the larger side. The converse of the Pythagorean theorem.
- **Note:** the proof of a locus must be in both directions. Distinguish between a theorem and its converse.
- **Common mistakes:** using congruence (SSA) without the angle being opposite the larger side.

### m10-5-04 Power functions and transformations (פונקציות חזקה)
- **Content:** power functions and their properties, even and odd functions, qualitative analysis of polynomials as a product of linear and quadratic factors, a root of even and odd multiplicity and its effect on the graph, graphical inequalities.
- **Transformations:** shift, stretch and shrink, reflection in the axes, absolute value. Forms like a(x−p)²+k and a√(x−p)+k and a/(x−p)+k.
- **Also:** operations on functions, and the function 1/f(x) and its relation to f, and composition of functions and the inverse function, and √(x²) = |x|.
- **Note:** the analysis here is qualitative. No calculation with ∞. Continuity in an intuitive way from the graphs.
- **Common mistakes:** f(x−p) moves right, not left. √(x²) = x instead of |x|.

### m10-5-05 Proportion and Thales' theorem (פרופורציה, משפט תאלס)
- **Content:** Thales' theorem and its converse and its extensions, and the angle bisector theorem in a triangle, and deriving new proportions from a given proportion.
- **Constructions:** dividing a segment into n equal parts or in a given ratio.
- **Common mistakes:** writing the proportion with sides that do not correspond.

### m10-5-06 Differentiation: the derivative (חשבון דיפרנציאלי)
- **Content:** the derivative as a rate of change, constant and non-constant rate of change, the tangent as a linear approximation, the definition of the derivative as the limit of rates of change (the slope of the secant), the derivative of polynomials.
- **Applications:** the equation of a tangent, a full investigation of a polynomial: domain, parity, intercepts, increasing and decreasing, extrema, a sketch. Investigation on a closed interval.
- **Note:** limits in an intuitive, numerical and graphical way. Examples from real life: speed, temperature, fuel consumption.
- **Common mistakes:** f'(x) = 0 is not always an extremum point. Forgetting the ends of the closed interval.

### m10-5-07 Similarity of triangles (דמיון משולשים)
- **Content:** the three similarity theorems. The ratio of perimeters, medians, altitudes and angle bisectors = the similarity ratio. The ratio of areas = the square of the similarity ratio.
- **Skills:** recognizing similar triangles and their corresponding sides, proving similarity, finding the similarity ratio, using similarity in a proof.
- **In the circle:** similarity from relations of angles. Two intersecting chords, a tangent and a secant are practiced as exercises, not as ready theorems.
- **Common mistakes:** the ratio of areas = the similarity ratio instead of its square.

### m10-5-08 Graph and derivative (גרף ונגזרת)
- **Content:** the relation between the graph of the function and the graph of its derivative in both directions, the derivative of a product and of a composition, an antiderivative of polynomials in a qualitative way (without the integral sign), extreme value problems for polynomials.
- **Note:** the graph of the function and the graph of its derivative are not drawn on the same axes. The derivative goes below it.
- **Common mistakes:** mixing up "the function is positive" and "the function is increasing".

### m10-5-09 The circle (המעגל)
- **Definitions:** radius, chord, diameter, arc, central angle, inscribed angle.
- **Theorems:** equal central angles ⇔ equal arcs and chords. The perpendicular from the center to a chord bisects it. The longer chord is closer to the center. The inscribed angle = half the central angle on the same arc. An inscribed angle on a diameter = 90°. The cyclic quadrilateral: the sum of two opposite angles is 180° and the converse.
- **Tangent:** perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency, and the converse. Two tangents from an external point are equal. A quadrilateral circumscribed around a circle: the sums of the opposite sides are equal. The angle between a tangent and a chord = the inscribed angle on the arc between them.
- **Constructions:** finding the center of a circle, drawing a tangent from a point on the circle and from a point outside it.
- **Common mistakes:** using "the inscribed angle = half the central angle" on different arcs.

### m10-5-10 The root function (פונקציית שורש)
- **Content:** introduction, pre-analysis, composition, the derivative and its applications, the equation of a tangent, extreme value problems.
- **Note:** radical equations must be checked by substitution after solving.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting the domain (what is under the root ≥ 0). Not checking the solutions after squaring.

### m10-5-11 The trigonometric functions (הפונקציות הטריגונומטריות)
- **Content:** the unit circle, wrapping the real numbers around the circle, sin is the y coordinate and cos is x. Periodicity, the graphs of sin and cos and their shifts, tan from the tangent to the unit circle, exact values for special angles, basic equations sin x = a with the general solution and the solution in an interval.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting the second solution of the trigonometric equation. Forgetting + 2πk in the general solution.

### m10-5-12 Trigonometry in the plane (טריגונומטריה במישור)
- **Content:** the link to the right triangle, the area of a triangle = ½ab·sinC (with a proof for acute and obtuse), the law of sines (משפט הסינוסים), the law of cosines (משפט הקוסינוסים), identities and basic equations.
- **Notes:** distinguish between an identity and an equation. Problems with missing data (a parameter) or extra data (check for a contradiction). Check the units and the size of the answer.
- **Common mistakes:** in the law of sines, forgetting the case in which there are two solutions for the angle.

### m10-5-13 The rational function (פונקציה רציונלית)
- **Content:** introduction, domain, vertical and horizontal asymptotes, a removable discontinuity, the quotient rule, applications, extreme value problems.
- **Note:** part of it may be completed in grade 11.
- **Common mistakes:** counting every zero in the denominator as an asymptote, even when it cancels.

## Moved to grade 11
Deepening in rational, root and trigonometric functions and their derivatives, and wider applications of the law of sines and the law of cosines.
