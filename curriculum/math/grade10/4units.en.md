# Math, Grade 10, 4 units (4 יח"ל)

Source: the official curriculum of the Ministry of Education for grade 10, 4 units, and the recommendations of the math inspector for 2026-2027 (תשפ"ז).
Total hours: at least 120 hours. Geometry 40, algebra and differential calculus 45, statistics and probability 35.

## Instructions for the teacher about this guide

- The order of the topics is a recommendation, and the teacher is allowed to change it. Ask the student how far the class has got.
- Teach only the topics in this guide.
- The answers are exact (roots without rounding) unless the question asked for an approximation.
- Angles in degrees only. Radians are not in the 4-unit curriculum for grade 10.
- The problems have few steps. If a problem is long, split it into small questions.
- Parameters in differential calculus are light in grade 10, and they expand in grade 11.
- Write the term in English, and the first time put the Hebrew term in parentheses.
- The common mistakes in this guide come from general teaching experience, not from the Ministry.

## The exam

The teaching recommendations for 2026-2027 name no exam form for grade 10. Bagrut 4 units: form (שאלון) 35471 in grade 11, and 35472 in grade 12.

## Topics from middle school that need to be reviewed
The topics that are usually not learned in grade 9, and are learned together with the new ones: relations between angles and sides in a triangle, the midsegment in a triangle and in a trapezoid, the ratio of areas in similar triangles, and systems of quadratic equations by the algebraic method.

## The topics in order

### m10-4-01 Analytic geometry (גאומטריה אנליטית)
- **Content:** points, the length of a segment, the midpoint of a segment (and finding one end from the other end and the midpoint), the slope as the ratio of the change in y to the change in x and the meaning of its sign, explicit and implicit equation, coinciding, parallel, intersecting and perpendicular lines, an equation from a slope and a point or from two points, intersections.
- **Together with:** a review of triangles and quadrilaterals from grade 9.
- **Out of scope here:** the definition of the slope as the tangent of the angle, which comes later (m10-4-05).
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting that a line parallel to the y axis has no slope.

### m10-4-02 Introduction to function analysis and algebra (קדם אנליזה)
- **Content:** graphing a function, intercepts, where it is positive and negative, increasing and decreasing, extrema without a derivative, the linear and the quadratic function graphically and algebraically.
- **Algebra:** factoring, laws of exponents, roots, first and second degree equations and inequalities, systems of two equations (up to second degree) and the meaning of the number of solutions graphically, rational equations, radical equations (up to two radicals), substitution (biquadratic equations).
- **Common mistakes:** in an inequality, forgetting to flip the sign when multiplying by a negative number. Mixing up solving an equation and solving an inequality.

### m10-4-03 Plane geometry: similarity (דמיון משולשים)
- **Content:** the midsegment in a triangle and in a trapezoid, the three similarity theorems (AA, SAS, SSS), the ratio of perimeters, altitudes, medians and angle bisectors = the similarity ratio, the ratio of areas = its square.
- **Common mistakes:** the ratio of areas = the similarity ratio instead of its square.

### m10-4-04 Functions: power functions and transformations (פונקציית חזקה)
- **Content:** the power function xⁿ, even and odd functions, transformations: f(x)+k, f(x−p), −f(x), f(−x), a·f(x), and the absolute value of a function.
- **Common mistakes:** f(x−p) shifts right, not left.

### m10-4-05 Trigonometry (טריגונומטריה)
- **Content:** sin and cos and tan of an acute angle in a right triangle, their values at 0° and 90°, tan and the slope, identities: tan = sin/cos, sin²+cos²=1, sin(90°−θ)=cos θ. Exact values for 30°, 45° and 60°. sin(180°−θ)=sin θ for an obtuse angle. Finding an angle with the calculator. Calculating sides, angles, perimeter and area, and area = ½·a·b·sinγ, and shapes that split into right triangles.
- **Common mistakes:** the calculator in radian mode. Mixing up the opposite side and the adjacent side.

### m10-4-06 Differential calculus (חשבון דיפרנציאלי)
- **Content:** constant and non-constant rate of change, the definition of the derivative at a point and its geometric meaning, the derivative of xⁿ, a sum, multiplying by a constant, a product and a composition (two steps only). The equation of a tangent. Investigating a polynomial: domain, parity, intercepts, increasing and decreasing, local and absolute extrema and at the ends, a sketch. The relation between the graph of the function and the graph of the derivative. Extreme value problems for polynomials.
- **Out of scope:** the second derivative.
- **Common mistakes:** f'(x) = 0 is not always an extremum. Forgetting to check the endpoints for an absolute extremum.

### m10-4-07 Plane geometry: special lines in a triangle (קווים מיוחדים במשולש)
- **Content:** the medians (meeting point and the 2:1 ratio, a median splits the triangle into two triangles of equal area, the median to the hypotenuse = half the hypotenuse), angle bisectors (locus, meeting point, the interior angle bisector theorem and its converse), perpendicular bisectors (locus, meeting point), altitudes (meeting point).
- **Common mistakes:** mixing up a median, an altitude and an angle bisector.

### m10-4-08 The root function (פונקציית שורש)
- **Content:** the domain of √(a polynomial up to second degree), its derivative, the derivative of a sum and of a root times a polynomial, the equation of a tangent, investigating the function, the relation between f and f', extreme value problems.
- **Note:** the wording of the curriculum about the degree limits when multiplying a root by a polynomial is not clear. Check with the teacher.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting the domain. Not checking the solution after squaring.

### m10-4-09 Statistics (סטטיסטיקה)
- **Content:** types of variables, presenting data (lists, frequency tables, charts), measures of center (mean, mode, median), quartiles and deciles, measures of spread (range, standard deviation).
- **Common mistakes:** finding the median without sorting the data.

### m10-4-10 Probability (הסתברות)
- **Content:** the sample space, events and operations on them, the laws of probability, conditional probability, a two-way table (2×2 or 2×3), a probability tree (two or three stages), independent and dependent events.
- **Common mistakes:** adding the probabilities of events that are not mutually exclusive. Mixing up P(A|B) and P(B|A).

### m10-4-11 Combining statistics and probability (שילוב סטטיסטיקה והסתברות)
Problems that combine the two topics.
