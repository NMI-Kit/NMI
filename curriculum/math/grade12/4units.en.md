# Math, Grade 12, 4 units (4 יח"ל)

Source: the recommendations of the math inspector for 2026-2027 (תשפ"ז).

## Instructions for the teacher about this guide

- The order of the topics is a recommendation, and the teacher is allowed to change it (for example sequences can come before vectors). Ask the student where the class has reached.
- Exponential and logarithmic equations and inequalities are needed only to serve differentiation and growth and decay problems, not as a topic on their own.
- Write the term in English, and the first time put the Hebrew term in parentheses.
- The common mistakes in this guide come from general teaching experience, not from the Ministry.

## The exam

Form (שאלון) 35472 in grade 12.

## The topics in order

### m12-4-01 Geometry in space (גאומטריה במרחב)
- **Content:** basic concepts of vectors in the geometric way: definition, notation, length, parallel (קולינאריים), equal and opposite vectors. Solids: a prism (ישרה ולאו דווקא, right or not necessarily), a pyramid, a right pyramid. Addition, subtraction, multiplying by a number and a linear combination (צירוף לינארי).

### m12-4-02 The exponential function (פונקציה מעריכית)
- **Content:** laws of exponents with fractional exponents, exponential equations, properties of the function and its graph, shift, stretch, shrink, reflection and absolute value, simple exponential inequalities (that reduce to a quadratic inequality at most).

### m12-4-03 The dot product, geometric method (מכפלה סקלרית)
- **Content:** the angle between two vectors, properties of the dot product, perpendicularity (ניצבות), lengths, angles and areas.

### m12-4-04 The logarithmic function (פונקציה לוגריתמית)
- **Content:** the logarithm and its laws, logarithmic equations and inequalities, properties of the function and its graph, its transformations.
- **Common mistakes:** log(a+b) = log a + log b (wrong). Forgetting the condition that what is inside the logarithm is positive.

### m12-4-05 A line or a vector perpendicular to a plane (ישר מאונך למישור)
- **Content:** definition and a sufficient condition, a right prism, the property of the altitude of a right pyramid, lengths, angles and areas with the dot product, calculating volumes (חישובי נפח).

### m12-4-06 Coordinate system in space and algebraic representation of vectors (מערכת צירים במרחב וייצוג אלגברי של וקטורים)
- **Content:** the algebraic representation of a vector in a coordinate system in space, and operations in the algebraic representation.

### m12-4-07 Operations on vectors and the dot product, algebraic method (פעולות על וקטורים ומכפלה סקלרית בדרך אלגברית)
- **Content:** the dot product in the algebraic way.

### m12-4-08 Mixed problems with vectors, both methods (שאלות משולבות בוקטורים בשתי הדרכים)
- **Content:** questions that combine the geometric and the algebraic way.

### m12-4-09 Growth and decay problems (בעיות גדילה ודעיכה)
- **Content:** processes that behave exponentially, algebraic and graphical representation, finding quantities and times, comparing processes, half-life (זמן מחצית חיים).
- **Common mistakes:** mixing up the growth rate (for example 5%) and the factor (1.05).

### m12-4-10 Sequences (סדרות)
- **Content:** two ways to define a sequence, an arithmetic sequence (סדרה חשבונית), a geometric sequence (סדרה הנדסית) and its infinite sum when it converges, combining the arithmetic and the geometric.

### m12-4-11 Differentiating the exponential function with base e (גזירת הפונקציה המעריכית בבסיס e)
- **Content:** derivatives with polynomials and rational functions, tangent, function investigation, the relation between the function and its derivative, operations on functions.

### m12-4-12 Differentiating the logarithmic function with base e (גזירת הפונקציה הלוגריתמית בבסיס e)
- **Content:** the same content as the previous item, for the logarithmic function.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting the domain of ln before the investigation.

### m12-4-13 Integral calculus (חשבון אינטגרלי)
- **Content:** the integral of e^x and 1/x and 1/f(x) when f is linear, combining with the integrals of grade 11, finding a function from its derivative and a point, areas with the definite integral.
