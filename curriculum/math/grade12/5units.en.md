# Math, Grade 12, 5 units (5 יח"ל)

Source: the recommendations of the math inspector for 2026-2027 (תשפ"ז), the new curriculum.
Note: the text of the source mixed some items of vectors with complex numbers. We arranged them here by topic. Check with the teacher.

## Instructions for the teacher about this guide

- The order of the topics is a recommendation, and the teacher is allowed to change it. Ask the student where the class has reached.
- The student knows all the material of grades 10 and 11, and you can build on it.
- The answers are exact, unless the question asked for an approximation.
- Write the term in English, and the first time put the Hebrew term in parentheses.
- The common mistakes in this guide come from general teaching experience, not from the Ministry.

## The exam

According to the table of exam rules for 2026-2027: the 5-unit exam forms (שאלון) are 35571 and 35572. Which grade takes which form is not verified (the teaching recommendations do not say), so ask the teacher.

## The topics in order

### m12-5-01 Analytic geometry (גאומטריה אנליטית)
- **Content:** dividing a segment in a given ratio, the distance of a point from a line, the distance between parallel lines, combining the tools of grade 10.
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting the absolute value in the distance formula.

### m12-5-02 Algebra: exponents and logarithms (אלגברה: חזקות ולוגריתמים)
- **Content:** the algebra of exponents and logarithms, growth and decay problems (בעיות גדילה ודעיכה).
- **Common mistakes:** log(a+b) = log a + log b (wrong). Forgetting the condition that what is inside the logarithm is positive.

### m12-5-03 The circle as a locus (המעגל כמקום גאומטרי)
- **Content:** finding the equation of a circle by completing the square, a tangent to a circle at a point on it, the relative positions of a line and a circle and of two circles, finding loci that are lines or circles.

### m12-5-04 Differentiation and integration of exponential and logarithmic functions (גזירה ואינטגרציה של פונקציות מעריכיות ולוגריתמיות)
- **Content:** differentiating exponential (מעריכיות) and logarithmic functions, their integral, the integral as an accumulated area, and combining all the previous types of functions.
- **Common mistakes:** the derivative of a^x = x·a^(x−1) (wrong). Forgetting the domain of ln.

### m12-5-05 Conic sections (חתכי חרוט)
- **Parabola (פרבולה):** a definition as a locus, the canonical equation, properties, relative positions with lines and circles, a tangent.
- **Ellipse (אליפסה):** two definitions, the canonical equation, properties, relative positions.
- **Hyperbola (היפרבולה):** a definition as a locus, the canonical equation, properties, relative positions, loci.
- **Details according to the curriculum document:**
  - **Parabola (פרבולה):** a locus, the canonical equation, properties, relative positions with a line and a circle, a tangent at a point on it.
  - **Ellipse (אליפסה):** a locus (two definitions), the canonical equation, properties, relative positions with a line and a circle whose center is on one of the axes.
  - **Hyperbola (היפרבולה):** a locus, the canonical equation, properties, relative positions with a line and a circle whose center is on one of the axes, and finding loci.

### m12-5-06 Vectors (וקטורים)
- **Content:** solids (a prism and a pyramid, מנסרה ופירמידה) and angles in space, vectors as arrows in the plane and in space, addition, subtraction and multiplying by a number, a linear combination, dividing a segment in a ratio, linear dependence (תלות לינארית) and the uniqueness of the representation.
- **The dot product (מכפלה סקלרית):** its properties, calculating lengths and angles, the algebraic representation of vectors.
- **Lines and planes in space:** a parametric equation of a line, representing a plane parametrically and with an equation, relative positions: two lines, a line and a plane, two planes.
- **Common mistakes:** mixing up parallel vectors and equal vectors.

### m12-5-07 Complex numbers (מספרים מרוכבים)
- **Content:** definition, algebraic form, equality, the four operations, the Gauss plane and the geometric meaning of addition, subtraction, absolute value and conjugate, square roots and solving quadratic equations, polar form (הצגה קוטבית) and converting between the forms, the geometric meaning of multiplication.
- **Common mistakes:** calculating the angle without noticing the quadrant.

### m12-5-08 De Moivre's theorem (משפט דה-מואבר)
- **Content:** roots of unity (שורשי יחידה), roots of order n and their geometric meaning, loci in complex numbers.

### m12-5-09 Distances and angles in space (מרחקים וזוויות במרחב)
- **Content:** the distance of a point from a line and from a plane, between parallel or skew lines, between a line and a parallel plane, between parallel planes. Angles between lines, between planes, and between a line and a plane. Mixed problems.
