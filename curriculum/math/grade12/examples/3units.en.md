# Exam-style examples, Grade 12, 3 units

Each example here is one question in the **format** of the exam questions: a story, parts a b c, and a "justify" part. The stories and the numbers are new, not copied from any exam. The numbers of the examples were checked with sympy (tests/kit/example_solutions_12.py).

## m12-3-01 The normal distribution and the standard score (התפלגות נורמלית וציון תקן)

**The story:** a factory fills bags of sugar, and the weight of a bag is normally distributed with a mean of 500 grams and a standard deviation of 5 grams.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the standard score of a bag that weighs 510 grams.

**Part b (5 marks):** approximately what percent of the bags weigh less than 510 grams? (use the table of the standard normal distribution)

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** a bag weighs 490 grams. What is its standard score, and what does it say about its place relative to the mean?

## m12-3-02 The quadratic model (מודל ריבועי)

**The story:** the profit of a store in shekels when it sells a product at a price of x ₪ is P(x) = -2x² + 40x - 150 (and negative means a loss).

**Part a (5 marks):** find the price that gives the highest profit, and find this profit.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the prices at which the profit is positive.

**Part c (4 marks) - justify:** explain in context the meaning of the vertex of the parabola of P.

## m12-3-03 Linear programming (תכנון לינארי)

**The story:** a bakery makes x boxes of cookies and y boxes of bread a day. The oven time is 100 machine hours: a box of cookies takes 2 and a box of bread takes 1. The workers work 70 hours: every box (cookies or bread) takes an hour. The profit is 30 ₪ for a box of cookies and 20 ₪ for a box of bread.

**Part a (5 marks):** write the constraints (with non-negativity) and the profit function.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the vertices of the region of solutions.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** find the largest profit. Why must we check all the vertices and not only one?

## m12-3-04 Spatial vision (ראייה מרחבית)

**The story:** a cylindrical water tank whose base has a radius of 3 m and whose height is 5 m. They want to paint its outer wall (the lateral part only) with paint whose price per square meter is 20 ₪.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the volume of the tank (leave π in the answer).

**Part b (5 marks):** find the lateral area (leave π in the answer).

**Part c (4 marks):** how much will the painting cost? (π = 3.14)

**Part d (4 marks) - justify:** a student calculated the total surface area of the cylinder instead of the lateral area. Find the total surface area, and say why it does not fit here.

## m12-3-05 Analytic geometry: the straight line (גאומטריה אנליטית: הקו הישר)

**The story:** a street passes through the two points A(1, 2) and B(5, 10) on a map, and another street whose equation is y = -x + 9.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the equation of the street AB.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the intersection point of the two streets.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** find the equation of a street parallel to AB that passes through the point (0, 4). Why do two parallel streets not intersect?
