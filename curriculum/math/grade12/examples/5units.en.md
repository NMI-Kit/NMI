# Exam-style examples, Grade 12, 5 units

Each example here is one question in the **format** of the exam questions: a story (or a function directly, like the differential calculus questions in the exam), parts a b c, and a "justify" part. The stories and the numbers are new, not copied from any exam. The numbers of the examples were checked with sympy (tests/kit/example_solutions_12.py).

## m12-5-01 Analytic geometry (גאומטריה אנליטית)

**The story:** a plot of land in the shape of a triangle with vertices A(0, 0) and B(8, 0) and C(2, 6) on a map (the unit is a meter).

**Part a (4 marks):** find the area of the triangle.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the equation of the median from C to the midpoint of AB.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** find the meeting point of the medians G, and check that it is on the median from part b.

## m12-5-02 Algebra: exponents and logarithms (אלגברה: חזקות ולוגריתמים)

**The story:** a question without a story, with two equations: 4^x - 5·2^x + 4 = 0, and log₃(x) + log₃(x + 6) = 3.

**Part a (5 marks):** solve the first equation (use the substitution u = 2^x).

**Part b (5 marks):** solve the second equation.

**Part c (4 marks) - justify:** why is one of the solutions of the second equation rejected?

## m12-5-03 The circle as a locus (המעגל כמקום גאומטרי)

**The story:** the points A(0, 0) and B(3, 0). The set S is all the points P whose distance from A is twice their distance from B.

**Part a (6 marks):** find the equation of S.

**Part b (4 marks):** show that S is a circle, and find its center and radius.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** check that the points (2, 0) and (6, 0) belong to S. Why must a locus be checked in both directions?

## m12-5-04 Differentiation and integration of exponential and logarithmic functions (גזירה ואינטגרציה של פונקציות מעריכיות ולוגריתמיות)

**The story:** a question without a story: the function f(x) = (ln x) / x.

**Part a (4 marks):** find the domain and the intersection point with the x axis.

**Part b (6 marks):** find f'(x) and the local extremum point and its type.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** find the area enclosed between f and the x axis from x = 1 to x = e. Why is f positive there?

## m12-5-05 Conic sections (חתכי חרוט)

**The story:** a hall in the shape of an ellipse whose equation is x²/25 + y²/9 = 1 (the unit is a meter), and its foci are F₁ and F₂.

**Part a (5 marks):** find the coordinates of the two foci.

**Part b (5 marks):** check that the point P(3, 12/5) is on the ellipse.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** find PF₁ and PF₂ and their sum. Why is the sum equal to 2a?

## m12-5-06 Vectors (וקטורים)

**The story:** on a three-dimensional model, the points A(1, 1, 1) and B(3, 2, 3) and C(5, 3, 5).

**Part a (4 marks):** find the vectors AB and AC.

**Part b (5 marks):** are the points A and B and C on one line? Justify with linear dependence.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** find the ratio AB:AC. Why is B the midpoint of AC?

## m12-5-07 Complex numbers (מספרים מרוכבים)

**The story:** a question without a story: the complex number z = 3 + 4i.

**Part a (4 marks):** find |z| and the conjugate, and find z · z̄.

**Part b (5 marks):** find 1/z in the form a + bi.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** solve the equation z² + 2z + 5 = 0. Why are the two solutions conjugates?

## m12-5-08 De Moivre's theorem (משפט דה-מואבר)

**The story:** a question without a story: the number (1 + i)⁸, and the equation z³ = 8.

**Part a (5 marks):** write 1 + i in polar form, and find (1 + i)⁸ with De Moivre's theorem.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the solutions of z³ = 8 in polar form and in algebraic form.

**Part c (4 marks) - justify:** why do the three solutions form the vertices of an equilateral triangle?

## m12-5-09 Distances and angles in space (מרחקים וזוויות במרחב)

**The story:** a right pyramid whose base is a square ABCD with a side of 6 m, and whose apex S is above the center of the base, and whose height is 4 m.

**Part a (5 marks):** find the angle between the lateral face and the base to the nearest tenth of a degree.

**Part b (5 marks):** find the distance from the center of the base to the lateral face.

**Part c (5 marks) - justify:** find the angle between the lateral edge SA and the base. Why is it smaller than the angle in part a?
