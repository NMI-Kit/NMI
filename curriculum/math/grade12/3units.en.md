# Math, Grade 12, 3 units (3 יח"ל)

Source: the recommendations of the math inspector for 2026-2027 (תשפ"ז), the new curriculum with clusters.

## Instructions for the teacher about this guide

- All the topics are from real life. Always use examples from real life.
- The order is a recommendation. Ask the student where the class has reached.
- Write the term in English, and the first time put the Hebrew term in parentheses.
- The common mistakes in this guide come from general teaching experience, not from the Ministry.

## The exam

Form (שאלון) 35372 in grade 12.

## Outside the 3-unit curriculum

Sequences, trigonometry in space, differential and integral calculus, and the analytic geometry of the circle (according to the new curriculum document). If the student asks about them, tell them honestly that they are not in the 3-unit curriculum.

## The topics in order

### m12-3-01 The normal distribution and the standard score (התפלגות נורמלית וציון תקן)
- **The cluster:** Science and Society, and Financial-economic.
- **Content:** estimating probabilities with the normal distribution, and the standard score (ציון תקן).
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting that the total area under the curve = 1. Mixing up the mark and the standard score.

### m12-3-02 The quadratic model (מודל ריבועי)
- **The cluster:** Science and Society, and Financial-economic.
- **Content:** processes and phenomena that behave like a quadratic function.
- **Common mistakes:** not explaining the meaning of the vertex of the parabola in context (the highest profit, the maximum height).

### m12-3-03 Linear programming (תכנון לינארי)
- **The cluster:** Financial-economic.
- **Content:** linear programming (the document only mentions the name).
- **Common mistakes:** forgetting the non-negativity constraints. Checking only one vertex of the region of solutions.

### m12-3-04 Spatial vision (ראייה מרחבית)
- **The cluster:** Orientation in the plane and in space.
- **Content:** spatial vision: a building of cubes, a number plan, and the views from the front and from the sides. Solids in space: volume, surface area and lateral area.
- **Common mistakes:** mixing up the total area and the lateral area.

### m12-3-05 Analytic geometry: the straight line (גאומטריה אנליטית: הקו הישר)
- **The cluster:** Financial-economic.
- **Content:** the straight line only, without the circle.

## Teaching tips from the Ministry
- One main topic in each period, and two topics in parallel are possible.
- After two topics are finished, a weekly review lesson on the topics that will come in the Bagrut.
